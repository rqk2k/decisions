# UNEP/CBD/COP/DEC/IX/24

9 October 2008

## IX/24. Gender Plan of Action

_The Conference of the Parties_

_Welcomes_ the development by the Executive Secretary of the Gender Plan of Action under the Convention on Biological Diversity, ([UNEP/CBD/COP/9/INF/12/Rev.1](https://www.cbd.int/documents/UNEP/CBD/COP/9/INF/12/Rev.1)), and _invites_ Parties to support the implementation of the Plan by the Secretariat.
